Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Opera Shalem website (operashalem.com) collects, why, and what rights you have. It is written to meet the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, as amended.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Opera Shalem is a cultural initiative in formation, based in Jerusalem, Israel. It has not yet been incorporated as a legal entity. Until it is, the controller responsible for personal data collected through this website is [TO CONFIRM: name of the responsible person or organisation, and a postal address].

You can reach us about anything in this policy at info@operashalem.com. When Opera Shalem is incorporated, we will update this policy to name the new entity.

2. What we collect

When you use the contact form or email us

We collect the details you give us: your name, email address, area of interest, your message, and anything else you choose to include. Giving us this information is voluntary and not required by law, but we cannot reply without a way to contact you.

When you visit the site

Like any website, our hosting provider automatically records technical information when pages are requested: your IP address, browser type, the pages you visit, the referring page, and the date and time. These server logs are used only to operate and secure the site and are kept for [TO CONFIRM: log retention period from hosting provider].

Fonts

Our pages load a typeface from an external font service (fonts.cdnfonts.com). To deliver the font, that service receives your IP address and browser details. We do not control how it uses that data.

What we don't do

  • We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data, or share it with anyone for their own marketing.
  • The social media icons are plain links. No data is sent to those platforms unless you click through, after which their own privacy policies apply.

3. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and continue the conversation you started, including about partnership. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, and, where you are discussing an arrangement with us, taking steps at your request before entering into an agreement (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) and 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep the website running and secure. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in operating a secure site (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To meet legal obligations, if any apply (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)).

We will not add you to any mailing list without your explicit consent.

4. Who receives your data

We share personal data only with service providers that process it on our behalf, and only as needed to provide their service:

  • Website hosting: [TO CONFIRM: hosting provider and country]
  • Email: [TO CONFIRM: email provider for info@operashalem.com and country]

We may also disclose data if required by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

5. International transfers

We are based in Israel, which the European Commission recognises as providing an adequate level of data protection. Where a service provider handles data in another country without such recognition, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and correspondence for as long as we need them to respond and to follow up on the conversation, and no longer than [TO CONFIRM: e.g. 24 months] after our last contact with you, unless we have an ongoing relationship or a legal reason to keep them longer.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected or completed;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to our use of your data, including where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw any consent you have given, at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@operashalem.com. We will respond within one month. We may need to confirm your identity first.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority: in the EU, the supervisory authority in your country of residence or work; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office; in Israel, the Privacy Protection Authority at the Ministry of Justice.

8. Children

This website is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and we limit access to enquiries to the people who need to respond to them. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our practices change, including when Opera Shalem is incorporated. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last revised.
